A scene found by rendering

Every point here is a gaussian that a path traced frame left behind. Each frame kept only what the scene did not already hold, so the scene grows as it is rendered: drag the timeline to see it as it stood after each frame. There is no mesh and no stored picture. Black is what no camera has seen yet.

The yellow frustum is the camera of the frame being taken in; the line is the path the frames were rendered along. Drag to turn about what is under the pointer (Alt: look from where you stand) · W A S D to move, Q E down and up · Shift fasterDrag to turn about what is under the finger · two fingers to move: pinch forward and back, drag sideways
