- **The Open Chess Set** (the pawn of the tutorials), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) by the Academy Software Foundation. Authored by Moeen and Mujtaba Sayed for the SideFX Houdini tutorial *Karma | A Beautiful Game*; contributed to the MaterialX project by SideFX, with a glTF conversion by Chris Rydalch and Mark Elendt and refinements by Jonathan Stone; the USD version by Pablo Delgado, reworked for the [usd-wg/assets](https://github.com/usd-wg/assets/tree/main/full_assets/OpenChessSet) repository; normal maps with baked displacement by Ed Mackey. The asset is not redistributed here: the tutorials download it from its repository. The images of the pawn on these pages are renders of it made with athenea.
